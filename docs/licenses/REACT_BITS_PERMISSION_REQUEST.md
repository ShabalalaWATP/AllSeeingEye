# Draft permission request: React Bits Evil Eye

Status: prepared at Alex's request on 11 October 2026; **not sent**.
Intended recipient: David Haz / the React Bits maintainers. Verify the designated
licensing contact before sending. This draft neither asserts permission nor
commits Alex to a fee or contract.

Subject: Permission to include Evil Eye in an AGPL-3.0-only application

Hello David,

I maintain [The All Seeing Eye](https://github.com/ShabalalaWATP/AllSeeingEye),
a self-hosted OSINT research application. I would like to release the project
under AGPL-3.0-only, with optional paid deployment assistance and support.

The application uses your Evil Eye TypeScript/Tailwind component from
https://reactbits.dev/r/EvilEye-TS-TW.json, retrieved on 4 September 2026.
Our [original import](https://github.com/ShabalalaWATP/AllSeeingEye/commit/6133c427)
and [current third-party notice](https://github.com/ShabalalaWATP/AllSeeingEye/blob/e1eabc933d2d08bcc7540cad990292cf1a2f742a/frontend/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md)
record the source, modifications and licence. We have retained your attribution.

We understand that the custom MIT + Commons Clause text permits use in an
application, while restricting sale, sublicensing and redistribution of the
components themselves. We are not assuming that it permits the AGPL distribution
we are proposing.

Would you be willing to provide an additional, AGPL-compatible licence for the
specific Evil Eye material included in this project, for example ordinary MIT
without the additional Commons Clause restriction? Alternatively, please let us
know whether you offer a suitable permission covering the same downstream rights.

The scope would include the original component, shader/noise/rendering code,
our adaptations for frame limiting, pause, resizing, lazy fallback and worker
rendering, and the PNG/WebP frame captures used as favicons, app icons and marks.
The intended permission must allow us and downstream recipients to use, modify
and distribute source and built versions of that material, including commercial
redistribution, extraction and reuse in other compatible projects as well as
this application and its forks. The grant must not be limited to use inside
All Seeing Eye. We would retain the agreed copyright and attribution notices.
A hosted demo would also use it.

If you can offer this, please identify the material/version and exact licence or
grant, and confirm that the permission covers downstream recipients and the
captured artwork. Please also identify any attribution or other conditions.
A paid-support offer does not buy rights to your component, and we would review
any proposed fee or terms before accepting them.

Thank you,
Alex Orr
