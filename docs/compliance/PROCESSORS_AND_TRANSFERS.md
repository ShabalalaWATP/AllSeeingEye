## Transfer review and change procedure

For each changed or newly enabled recipient, record privately:

1. The activity IDs, minimum data, purpose, recipient's actual legal role and
   country of processing/support, including subprocessors and onward access.
2. The applicable Article 28 agreement where a processor is used. For transfers
   outside the EEA, record the applicable adequacy decision or other Chapter V
   safeguard, its parties/scope/version and any required transfer assessment.
   A provider's generic policy alone is not a completed installation assessment.
3. Necessary supplementary controls, retention/deletion, rights handling and the
   decision owner/date. Restrict or stop the affected use when the conditions
   cannot be established; do not treat a public source or consent toggle as a
   universal exemption.
4. The changed notice/register, tested destination and release evidence. Record
   uncertainty explicitly; do not put private contracts, keys, hosts or research
   content in a public PR.

The [Irish authority's transfer guidance](https://www.dataprotection.ie/en/organisations/international-transfers/transfers-personal-data-third-countries-or-international-organisations)
explains the EEA/third-country distinction and available safeguards. KAN-185 owns
the source-specific risk/necessity decisions and outstanding recipient assessments.
This inventory closes neither those decisions nor a legal review by implication.
